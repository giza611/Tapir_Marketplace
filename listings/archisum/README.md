# archiSum

archiSum is based on the Sum area Python script shared by Mathias Jonathan on the Graphisoft Community forum. We turned the idea into a proper Archicad add-on so the community can use it without needing to run Python, and we're releasing it for free as a way to give back to the original author and to everyone who shares their work in the community.

## What it does

archiSum groups by zone number, zone ID, or any custom property you use on zones. The target property is also configurable: write totals to whichever zone property fits your schedule.

- Group zones by zone number, ID, or any custom property
- Sum zone areas per apartment with configurable decimal precision
- Measured area and calculated area modes
- Multi-level filtering by layer or zone category
- Additional property-based filter for fine-grained control
- Write area totals back to a custom zone property
- Summary dialog with zone tree view and exclusion details
- Zone highlighting to preview affected zones before applying
- Run silently, with popup, or full verification mode
- Full undo support for all property changes

## Why you would use it

Area schedules are contractual on most projects. Finding out at tender stage
that two rooms share a number, or that a zone was stretched and nobody updated
its target, is expensive. Running this before every issue is cheap.
