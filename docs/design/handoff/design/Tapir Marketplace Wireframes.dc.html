WHAT IT DOES
Sums the net floor area of every selected zone and writes the total into a chosen property, so schedules stay honest after a plan change. Handles nested zones, skips zones marked as excluded, and reports anything it could not read.
Requires the Tapir add-on. Tested on Windows and macOS. Run it from the Tapir command palette.
